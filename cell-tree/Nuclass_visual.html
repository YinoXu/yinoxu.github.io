Measurement definitions and uncertainty

Core = interior depth ≥50% of maximum candidate depth; rim = remaining interior. Exterior rings are assigned to the nearest TLS, avoiding overlap double counting. Graph features use kNN12 with 25/50/100 µm distance caps, not complete-radius edge-corrected point-process statistics. Soft counts sum probabilities; high-confidence hard counts require coarse-group maximum probability ≥0.7. Entropy and soft–hard sensitivity quantify model-output ambiguity, not calibrated correctness.

Epithelial is not a confirmed malignancy label; stroma is not a CAF subtype; endothelium is not HEV; T is not Tfh. NuClass uses morphology to predict identities, so identity-dependent morphology is partly classifier-conditioned. The TLS detector is a rule/spatial pipeline with H&E boundary refinement, not a separately trained neural TLS segmenter.

Spatial context and uncertainty, side by side.

Read composition together with core/rim placement, exterior gradients, cell-pair neighborhoods and label uncertainty. A probability-sensitive spatial feature can reflect tissue biology, classifier ambiguity or both.

Exterior bands are assigned to the nearest TLS so overlapping neighborhoods do not double-count cells. Epithelial predictions do not identify malignancy; endothelium is not an HEV annotation; T-cell labels do not identify Tfh. These distinctions matter when interpreting the spatial hypotheses.

The most promising signals concern how TLS differ within a patient.

Interfaces, endothelial hotspots, myeloid positioning and exterior plasma concentration are the leading interpretable prognostic hypotheses.

The interface contrast is the fraction of T–epithelial graph edges crossing a TLS boundary minus the corresponding T–stromal fraction. Its IQR across a patient’s TLS measures heterogeneity. A graph edge is a predicted cell-pair relationship, not evidence of direct molecular interaction.

What Codex actually changed in this batch

Round1 expanded the original 964-feature program to 117 additional patients and added 24 TLS definitions plus within-patient tail summaries. Training feedback emphasized interface heterogeneity, plasma–stroma variability and T–myeloid range. Round2 then added eight TLS definitions, including 25/100 µm contrasts and interface–graph coupling, plus robust patient summaries.

Total additions: 32 TLS definitions and 86 patient-level feature definitions, plus six new candidate-pattern fractions and a diversity measure. The final candidate pool contains 1,057 feature columns; related distances, summaries and probability variants are not independent biological hypotheses. Every fitted final design has at most50 transformed columns. These were two actual Codex definition rounds in this session, with zero external LLM API calls; this is not a continuously running autonomous LLM service.
