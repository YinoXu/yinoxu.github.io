Measurement definitions and uncertainty

Core = interior depth ≥50% of maximum candidate depth; rim = remaining interior. Exterior rings are assigned to the nearest TLS, avoiding overlap double counting. Graph features use kNN12 with 25/50/100 µm distance caps, not complete-radius edge-corrected point-process statistics. Soft counts sum probabilities; high-confidence hard counts require coarse-group maximum probability ≥0.7. Entropy and soft–hard sensitivity quantify model-output ambiguity, not calibrated correctness.

Epithelial is not a confirmed malignancy label; stroma is not a CAF subtype; endothelium is not HEV; T is not Tfh. NuClass uses morphology to predict identities, so identity-dependent morphology is partly classifier-conditioned. The TLS detector is a rule/spatial pipeline with H&E boundary refinement, not a separately trained neural TLS segmenter.

03 / NEW, INTERPRETABLE HYPOTHESES

The most promising signals concern how TLS differ within a patient.

Mean cell composition is only one view. This batch nominates interfaces, localized endothelial/plasma concentrations, myeloid positioning and measurement sensitivity as richer hypotheses.

The HRs below are training-side, post-selection associations, adjusted for clinical variables and conventional TLS burden. Their nominal intervals do not account for the feature search; these are prioritized hypotheses, not confirmed biomarkers.

Interface heterogeneity

A patient’s TLS differ in whether T-cell boundary contacts involve epithelial or stromal predictions. This repeatedly entered feature selection and prompted new 25/100 µm definitions.

Plasma-cell concentration outside TLS

Uncertainty as a tissue-associated signal

Exact feature definitions, adjustment and all selected association results

The interface contrast is the fraction of T–epithelial graph edges crossing a TLS boundary minus the corresponding T–stromal fraction. Its IQR across a patient’s TLS measures heterogeneity. A graph edge is a predicted cell-pair relationship, not evidence of direct molecular interaction.

Cancer-stratified Efron Cox models adjust for age, sex, stageII, stageIII/IV combined, TLS presence, density and area fraction. StageIII/IV pooling was specified before the new comparisons to avoid the previous isolated-stageIV instability. Sixty-three selected association attempts yielded 62 estimable fits. What Codex actually changed in this batch

Round1 expanded the original 964-feature program to 117 additional patients and added 24 TLS definitions plus within-patient tail summaries. Training feedback emphasized interface heterogeneity, plasma–stroma variability and T–myeloid range. Round2 then added eight TLS definitions, including 25/100 µm contrasts and interface–graph coupling, plus robust patient summaries.

Total additions: 32 TLS definitions and 86 patient-level feature definitions, plus six new candidate-pattern fractions and a diversity measure. The final candidate pool contains 1,057 feature columns; related distances, summaries and probability variants are not independent biological hypotheses. Every fitted final design has at most50 transformed columns. These were two actual Codex definition rounds in this session, with zero external LLM API calls; this is not a continuously running autonomous LLM service.

How patterns were learned, what their names mean and what remains unproven

Only training-side TLS enter discovery. At most30 TLS per training patient contribute to fitting, preventing the largest TLS-bearing patients from dominating. Training-only imputation, clipping, scaling and six PCA axes precede diagonal Gaussian mixtures. BIC selects six among the tested range3–6; six is the edge of that range, so this does not establish a natural, unique number of biological types. All6,706 TLS are then assigned using the frozen model.

The descriptive names reflect relative median feature profiles. P1 shows a broad cell-size shift across several predicted identities, which could reflect segmentation or technical scaling as well as biology. P4 has relatively more stromal-oriented than epithelial-oriented boundary contacts. P5 combines radial organization with plasma–stromal coupling. No cluster is named centroblast, centrocyte or germinal center; those require independent identity and pathology evidence.

These patterns overlap with predicted E/P/S and have not yet demonstrated incremental prognosis as categorical types. Their value here is to organize new, inspectable hypotheses. Candidate assignments · Profiles

08 / METHODS & RESEARCH CONTEXT

A focused view of the leads, with the full evidence underneath.

The public narrative highlights new, interpretable leads. Full attempt tables and definitions remain downloadable. Ongoing whole-cohort inference will increase data availability; this page reports the completed196-patient batch, not results for all927 queued patients.

Primary papers and visual-design reference

Original figures use white backgrounds, lettered panels, patient dots, restrained blue/purple/pink/teal colors, forest intervals and survival risk tables. The visual reference was Hist2Prot, Nature Cancer (2026); its public main figures3,6 and8 were inspected. No publication figure is reproduced here. Full article text was not available through the public preview.

Conceptual context: MorphLink for morphology–spatial molecular links; SPARK for agent-supported pathology concepts; Squidpy for spatial analysis. These sources motivate hypotheses rather than validate the present measurements.
